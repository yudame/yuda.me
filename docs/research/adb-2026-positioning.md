# ADB 2026 Positioning Brief

**Status:** Internal reference. Vocabulary source of truth for downstream copy work (issues #6–#9).
**Last refreshed:** 2026-05-08
**Tone for downstream consumers:** Echo ADB's own register. Do not paraphrase into something slicker. If a claim is not in this brief with a source, do not put it on the site.

## 1. ADB 2026 digital priority themes

### 1.1 Digital transformation as one of five strategic focus areas

ADB's [Work Program and Budget Framework, 2026–2028](https://www.adb.org/documents/work-program-and-budget-framework-2026-2028) (October 2025) elevates digital transformation to one of five strategic priorities alongside climate action, private sector development, regional cooperation and public goods, and resilience and empowerment. The 2026–2028 program totals USD 98.6 billion in lending and grant commitments — roughly 240 projects per year — enabled by capital management reforms since 2023. For vendor copy, "five strategic priorities" with digital as a peer (not a sub-theme) is the framing to preserve.

Sources: [Work Program and Budget Framework 2026–2028](https://www.adb.org/documents/work-program-and-budget-framework-2026-2028) · [Budget of the Asian Development Bank for 2026](https://www.adb.org/documents/budget-asian-development-bank-2026)

### 1.2 Inclusive digital transformation under Strategy 2030

The canonical framing document is the [Strategy 2030 Digital Technology Directional Guide: Supporting Inclusive Digital Transformation for Asia and the Pacific](https://www.adb.org/documents/strategy-2030-digital-technology-directional-guide). It positions ADB's digital work as helping DMCs "overcome development challenges, achieve Sustainable Development Goals, and reach Paris Agreement targets — by promoting an inclusive digital transformation." A vendor brief that does not cite this guide reads as uninformed; "inclusive digital transformation" is the strategy-level header phrase.

Source: [Strategy 2030 Digital Technology Directional Guide](https://www.adb.org/documents/strategy-2030-digital-technology-directional-guide) · [Summary edition](https://www.adb.org/publications/digital-technology-development-directional-guide-summary)

### 1.3 Digital public infrastructure (DPI) as the dominant investment category

DPI — digital ID, digital payments, and data exchange layers — is ADB's top-shelf phrase for the rails it currently funds. ADB's Digital Sector Office (DIG, within Sectors Department 2) explicitly scopes its work to "digital public infrastructure (DPI), cloud and data platforms, cybersecurity solutions, AI-enabled systems, GovTech platforms, and sectoral digital systems." The Fiji national digital ID and eKYC engagement is the cleanest live exemplar: ADB cites that DPI "can reduce administrative costs by up to 50%, improve service delivery by 30%–40%, and expand financial inclusion by up to 60%" in low- and middle-income countries.

Sources: [ADB Supports Digitalization and Financial Inclusion in Fiji](https://www.adb.org/news/adb-supports-digitalization-and-financial-inclusion-fiji) · [Digital Technology Specialist (eGovernment and DPI) job posting](https://www.adb.org/careers/250658) · [Senior Digital Transformation Specialist posting](https://www.adb.org/careers/260344)

### 1.4 AI-enabled public services and the AIxADB program

ADB's 2025–2026 AI register is "AI-enabled public services" / "AI-enabled systems," not "AI-first" or "AI-native." The [AIxADB 2025](https://www.adb.org/news/events/aixadb-2025) program (second annual edition, themed "Harnessing AI for Public Sector Innovation and Fiscal Resilience") and the regional TA project [Empowering ADB Developing Member Countries through Artificial Intelligence Solutions (58497-001)](https://www.adb.org/projects/58497-001/main) operationalize this. Project 58497-001 explicitly targets the Pacific region, Mongolia, Cambodia, Bangladesh, Georgia, Nepal, Pakistan, and Uzbekistan, and aims to place AI solutions in at least four ministries/agencies by 2028 — using the "ADB AI Sandbox" to test MVPs and proofs of concept.

Sources: [AIxADB 2025](https://www.adb.org/news/events/aixadb-2025) · [58497-001 Empowering DMCs through AI Solutions](https://www.adb.org/projects/58497-001/main) · [AI Can Help Build a Smarter Public Sector (ADB blog)](https://blogs.adb.org/blog/ai-can-help-build-smarter-public-sector-across-asia-and-pacific)

### 1.5 Pacific digital financial inclusion and connectivity

ADB explicitly frames Pacific financial exclusion as solvable through DPI plus digital financial services (DFS), and connects this to physical connectivity investment. [Digital Financial Services in the Pacific](https://www.adb.org/publications/digital-financial-services-pacific) is the named publication. ADB works with the Pacific's 14 developing member countries (Cook Islands, Fiji, Kiribati, Marshall Islands, Federated States of Micronesia, Nauru, Niue, Palau, Papua New Guinea, Samoa, Solomon Islands, Tonga, Tuvalu, Vanuatu). Recent Pacific connectivity work includes the Vanuatu Tamtam submarine cable (second cable, dual-use as tsunami/earthquake early-warning system) and the Tonga–Fiji submarine cable. The 2026 Annual Meeting announced an [Asia–Pacific Digital Highway](https://www.adb.org/news/adb-launches-70-billion-push-connect-asia-power-grids-digital-networks) program to mobilize ~USD 20 billion by 2035 for digital corridors, data infrastructure, and "AI-ready economies."

Sources: [ADB in the Pacific](https://www.adb.org/where-we-work/pacific) · [Digital Financial Services in the Pacific](https://www.adb.org/publications/digital-financial-services-pacific) · [Supporting Inclusive and Sustainable Finance in the Pacific (57169-001)](https://www.adb.org/projects/57169-001/main) · [ADB Launches $70 Billion Push to Connect Asia's Power Grids, Digital Networks](https://www.adb.org/news/adb-launches-70-billion-push-connect-asia-power-grids-digital-networks)

### 1.6 "Harnessing digital transformation for good" as 2025 flagship register

ADB's [Asian Development Policy Report 2025](https://www.adb.org/publications/asian-development-policy-report-2025), titled *Harnessing Digital Transformation for Good*, sets the bank's tonal register for 2025–2026: digital is framed as an inclusion and sustainability lever, not a productivity story. Headline findings include a 13-percentage-point urban-rural internet usage gap, 38% faster mobile download speeds in urban areas, and a finding that a 1% increase in firm digitalization raises the firm's environmental score by ~1%. The report's three-pillar policy frame (national digital strategy integrating inclusion + sustainability; locally tailored policies; cross-cutting governance) is the policy spine downstream copy can lean against.

Sources: [Asian Development Policy Report 2025](https://www.adb.org/publications/asian-development-policy-report-2025) · [PDF of full report](https://www.adb.org/sites/default/files/publication/1050686/asian-development-policy-report-2025.pdf) · [Digitalization Can Reduce Persistent Inequality in Asia and the Pacific](https://www.adb.org/news/digitalization-can-reduce-persistent-inequality-asia-and-pacific)

## 2. Vocabulary cheat-sheet

Format: `**Term** — gloss of what it signals or when ADB uses it. (source)`

- **Strategy 2030** — ADB's overarching corporate strategy through 2030; all sector framing (including digital) ladders to it. ([Strategy 2030 Digital Technology Directional Guide](https://www.adb.org/documents/strategy-2030-digital-technology-directional-guide))
- **Inclusive digital transformation** — ADB's strategy-level header phrase for the digital agenda; preserve verbatim. ([Strategy 2030 Digital Technology Directional Guide](https://www.adb.org/documents/strategy-2030-digital-technology-directional-guide))
- **Digital public infrastructure (DPI)** — Investment category covering digital ID, digital payments, and data-exchange layers; the dominant 2025–2026 phrase for the foundational rails ADB funds. ([Fiji digitalization news release](https://www.adb.org/news/adb-supports-digitalization-and-financial-inclusion-fiji))
- **Digital financial services (DFS)** — Mobile money, digital payments, and adjacent service rails for the unbanked; primary Pacific lever. ([Digital Financial Services in the Pacific](https://www.adb.org/publications/digital-financial-services-pacific))
- **AI-enabled public services** — ADB's preferred AI-in-government register; pair-phrase is "AI-enabled systems." Not "AI-first," not "AI-native." ([Senior Digital Transformation Specialist posting](https://www.adb.org/careers/260344) · [AIxADB 2025](https://www.adb.org/news/events/aixadb-2025))
- **AIxADB** — Annual flagship AI program / forum; second edition in 2025, themed "Harnessing AI for Public Sector Innovation and Fiscal Resilience." Use for credibility signal. ([AIxADB 2025](https://www.adb.org/news/events/aixadb-2025))
- **ADB AI Sandbox** — Mechanism under TA 58497-001 for developing/testing AI MVPs and proofs of concept across DMCs. ([58497-001 project page](https://www.adb.org/projects/58497-001/main))
- **Developing Member Country (DMC)** — ADB's term for the 50 regional borrower countries; vendor copy targeting ADB should use "DMC" once and gloss it once. ([About ADB](https://www.adb.org/who-we-are/about))
- **Digital ID** — National identity rails; in ADB context usually paired with "eKYC" and DPI. Fiji is the live exemplar. ([ADB Supports Digitalization and Financial Inclusion in Fiji](https://www.adb.org/news/adb-supports-digitalization-and-financial-inclusion-fiji))
- **eKYC** — Electronic Know Your Customer; the verification rail layered on a digital ID for financial inclusion. ([Fiji digitalization news release](https://www.adb.org/news/adb-supports-digitalization-and-financial-inclusion-fiji))
- **Pacific subregion / Pacific DMCs** — ADB's term for its 14 Pacific developing member countries (Cook Islands through Vanuatu). Use "Pacific subregion" or "Pacific DMCs," not "Pacific Islands" alone. ([ADB in the Pacific](https://www.adb.org/where-we-work/pacific))
- **GovTech** — Public-sector digital platforms category; sits alongside DPI in DIG's scope. ([Senior Digital Transformation Specialist posting](https://www.adb.org/careers/260344))
- **Digital Sector Office (DIG)** — ADB's central digital unit, housed in Sectors Department 2 (SD2); leads mainstreaming of digital in operations. ([Senior Digital Transformation Specialist posting](https://www.adb.org/careers/260344))
- **Asia–Pacific Digital Highway** — 2026 ADB initiative to mobilize ~USD 20 billion by 2035 for digital corridors, subsea/terrestrial fiber, data centers, "AI-ready economies." Newest top-of-funnel phrase. ([ADB news release](https://www.adb.org/news/adb-launches-70-billion-push-connect-asia-power-grids-digital-networks))
- **"Harnessing digital transformation for good"** — Title of the 2025 flagship policy report; sets a mission-driven, inclusion-and-sustainability tonal register. ([Asian Development Policy Report 2025](https://www.adb.org/publications/asian-development-policy-report-2025))
- **Five strategic priorities / focus areas** — Climate action, private sector development, regional cooperation and public goods, digital transformation, resilience and empowerment. Use this list verbatim when situating digital. ([Work Program and Budget Framework 2026–2028](https://www.adb.org/documents/work-program-and-budget-framework-2026-2028))
- **Country Partnership Strategy (CPS)** — Bilateral programming document (e.g., Fiji CPS 2024–2028); vendor proposals reference the relevant CPS to show alignment. ([Fiji digitalization news release](https://www.adb.org/news/adb-supports-digitalization-and-financial-inclusion-fiji))
- **Sovereign / nonsovereign operations** — ADB's lending categories. 2026–2028 split: USD 78.7B sovereign, USD 19.9B nonsovereign. Vendor copy rarely needs both, but "sovereign operations" is the right phrase for government-side projects. ([Work Program and Budget Framework 2026–2028](https://www.adb.org/documents/work-program-and-budget-framework-2026-2028))
- **Digital divides** — ADB's 2025 framing for inclusion gaps (urban/rural internet usage, speed, gender, MSME). Use plural — "divides," not "the divide." ([Asian Development Policy Report 2025](https://www.adb.org/sites/default/files/publication/1050686/asian-development-policy-report-2025.pdf))
- **Digital public goods** — Reusable, open digital components/AI MVPs; appears in TA 58497-001 alongside DPI. ([58497-001 project page](https://www.adb.org/projects/58497-001/main))

## 3. Yudame-to-ADB angle map

Honest threading of existing Yudame work into the §1 themes. "No credible angle" appears where it belongs.

### 3.1 → Five focus areas / digital as peer
**Angle:** Use ADB's verbatim "five strategic priorities" framing in any homepage paragraph that situates Yudame against ADB's current mandate. Yudame can credibly position as a delivery partner *within* the digital priority — not as a strategic advisor on the priority mix.

### 3.2 → Inclusive digital transformation (Strategy 2030)
**Angle:** Yudame's product-engineering practice can ladder to "inclusive digital transformation" via accessibility, low-bandwidth/edge-tolerant patterns, and multilingual UX work — *if* shipped projects support that claim. If the Yudame portfolio does not include an accessibility/inclusion case study, this angle is aspirational and should be soft-pedaled in copy until issue #7 (case studies) lands a real one.

### 3.3 → Digital public infrastructure (DPI)
**No credible direct angle.** Yudame has not delivered a DPI rail (digital ID, payment switch, data exchange). Forcing this claim will be sniffed out. Honest framing: position Yudame as a *DPI-adjacent application layer* partner — building citizen-facing services that sit on top of someone else's DPI — rather than a DPI integrator. Flag this gap to issue #7 / #9 planners.

### 3.4 → AI-enabled public services / AIxADB
**Angle:** Strongest theme-fit. Yudame's "AI-forward" practice maps directly onto "AI-enabled public services" with a register adjustment (see §4). Concrete threading points: (a) MVP and proof-of-concept delivery cadence aligns with the ADB AI Sandbox model under TA 58497-001; (b) Yudame's developer-AI tooling discipline is a credible answer to "how do you ship AI features without burning the budget on rework." Do not overclaim public-sector deployment experience until a case study exists.

### 3.5 → Pacific digital financial inclusion and connectivity
**Angle:** Yudame's Pacific telecom engagement (issue #9 / placeholder practice page) is the natural wedge for the Pacific DFS theme. Honest framing today: "Yudame is engaged with a Pacific telecom operator" is a defensible factual claim; "Yudame has a Pacific DFS practice" is not — treat the practice page as forward-looking until the engagement produces a case study. Thailand HQ + DMC-resident-vendor framing reinforces regional credibility (Thailand is a DMC) but is not a substitute for Pacific-specific delivery proof.

### 3.6 → "Harnessing digital transformation for good"
**Use with care.** This register is mission-driven and somewhat NGO-flavored. Yudame can echo it in selected ADB-facing surfaces (a security/compliance page, a Pacific practice page) but blanket-applying it to the homepage risks tonal whiplash for non-ADB audiences. Recommend: use "harnessing" / "for good" sparingly, in ADB-targeted sections only.

## 4. Vocabulary adjustments to consider

Downstream copywriters (issues #6–#9): apply these register shifts when writing for ADB-adjacent surfaces.

- **"AI-forward" → "AI-enabled."** Yudame's current homepage and meta description use "AI-forward development" ([src/index.html](../../src/index.html), lines 9 and 590). ADB's 2025–2026 register is "AI-enabled public services" / "AI-enabled systems." Keep "AI-forward" for the general-market homepage; substitute "AI-enabled" anywhere copy is meant to read to an ADB task manager. ([AIxADB 2025](https://www.adb.org/news/events/aixadb-2025) · [Senior Digital Transformation Specialist posting](https://www.adb.org/careers/260344))
- **"Digital transformation" alone — fine. "Inclusive digital transformation" — preferred when ADB Strategy 2030 framing is in scope.** ([Strategy 2030 Digital Technology Directional Guide](https://www.adb.org/documents/strategy-2030-digital-technology-directional-guide))
- **"Pacific Islands" → "Pacific subregion" / "Pacific DMCs"** when writing for ADB readers. The bank's framing is administrative, not geographic. ([ADB in the Pacific](https://www.adb.org/where-we-work/pacific))
- **"MVP" — keep, but pair with "proof of concept"** when writing about AI work: ADB's TA 58497-001 uses the doublet "MVPs and proofs of concept." ([58497-001 project page](https://www.adb.org/projects/58497-001/main))
- **Avoid "AI-first," "AI-native."** Not in ADB's register. Substitute "AI-enabled."
- **"Customer" → "citizen" / "user"** for public-sector framings. DPI and GovTech copy uses "citizen" and "user," not "customer." ([Senior Digital Transformation Specialist posting](https://www.adb.org/careers/260344))

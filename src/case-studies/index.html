Internal practice

Compressed evidence-gathering cycles from days to hours for a small research team — without expanding headcount.

AI-forward research tooling — Yudame internal

Context

Yudame's own research practice needed to move faster on evidence-gathering for sector briefs and vendor positioning work. A two-person team was the bottleneck on every report.

What we did

Built an AI-forward tooling layer that pulls primary sources, drafts structured summaries, and flags gaps for human review. Designed it as a workflow the team could operate, audit, and extend on their own.

Result

Research cycle time dropped from multi-day to same-day on a typical brief. The capability is owned in-house: the team runs it, tunes it, and knows where its limits are. No external dependency locked in.

Capabilities AI-forward development · context engineering · knowledge transfer

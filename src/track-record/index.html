Cyndra AI cyndra.ai CTO · 2026–present

Autonomous AI employees on customer-owned hardware — deployed in hours, owned from day one.

Context

Businesses are ready for AI team members, but there was no way to hire one. Cyndra provisions autonomous AI employees that run on hardware the customer owns — not a subscription to someone else's cloud.

What we did

Yudame holds the CTO seat. We built the provisioning stack: configurable personas with persistent memory and a local skill layer, working through the same email, chat, and app tools as the human staff they join.

Simplenight simplenight.com Cache Architect · 2021–2024

Pricing for 700,000+ hotels worldwide, kept fresh and served in under 100 milliseconds.

Context

A global travel distribution platform whose hotel rates change constantly, across 191 countries. The existing system couldn't keep pricing fresh at that scale.

What we did

Designed a Redis caching layer between GDS data feeds and the consumer-facing API — real-time ETL processing 70GB of rates and availability per minute, with geospatial indexing across the full inventory.

Result

API response times dropped 85% to under 100ms, keeping displayed prices accurate and booking flows fast enough to convert.
